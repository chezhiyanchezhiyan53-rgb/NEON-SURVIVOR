HOW TO PLAY

Movement

Pilot your ship with WASD or arrow keys. Stay inside the neon arena and keep moving — standing still is a death sentence.

Shooting

Aim with the mouse and click to fire. On mobile, hold SHOOT. Projectiles travel toward your aim point.

Weapons

1 Normal — balanced single shot.
2 Spread — five bolts, lower damage each.
3 Laser — piercing beam, longer cooldown.

Power-ups

Grab glowing pickups: Shield, Slow Motion, Double Score, Rapid Fire, and Health. Only one timed effect stacks visually on the HUD at a time, but durations are independent.

Enemies

Basics chase. Fast units rush. Tanks soak hits. Shooters keep range. Mix changes as levels rise.

Scoring

Kills, coins, survival time, bosses, and power-ups all add score. Chain kills for a combo multiplier. Taking damage resets combo.

Bosses

Every 5 levels a boss arrives. Learn its patterns, drain the health bar, and collect the reward drop.

Controls

Desktop: WASD, mouse, Space, 1-3, P. Mobile: left joystick, SHOOT, SHIELD, PAUSE. Mute with the speaker button.
